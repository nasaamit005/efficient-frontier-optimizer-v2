What this chart measures

Compares the frequency and shape of daily asset returns.

Why it matters for portfolio research

Helps reveal dispersion and tail behavior that averages can conceal.

Construction and formula

Histograms group daily returns into bins; skewness, excess kurtosis and empirical quantiles are calculated from observed returns.

Calculated finding

Use the Asset Metrics sheet to compare skewness, excess kurtosis, worst daily return and historical 1% and 5% quantiles for each asset.

Assumptions and limitations

Histogram shape depends on binning. Tail quantiles and higher moments are uncertain estimates, especially with limited tail observations.

Annualization convention: 252 observations per year. Assumed annual risk-free rate: 2.00%. Results describe the supplied historical sample and are not forecasts or investment advice.
