What this chart measures

Compares Pearson correlations across three chronological periods.

Why it matters for portfolio research

Shows how historical co-movement may vary across the sample and informs diversification analysis.

Construction and formula

Pearson correlation = covariance of two return series / product of their standard deviations.

Calculated finding

Average pairwise correlation was lowest in the recent period (0.370) and highest in the middle period (0.563).

Assumptions and limitations

These are chronological thirds, not statistically estimated market regimes. Correlation does not establish causation.

Annualization convention: 252 observations per year. Assumed annual risk-free rate: 2.00%. Results describe the supplied historical sample and are not forecasts or investment advice.
