What this chart measures

Tracks each asset's decline from its own previous running price peak.

Why it matters for portfolio research

Shows the depth and timing of historical losses beyond volatility alone.

Construction and formula

Drawdown at date t = price at t / highest price observed through t − 1.

Calculated finding

META had the deepest observed maximum drawdown (-76.74%).

Assumptions and limitations

Drawdowns depend on sample start and end dates. Historical drawdown does not quantify the probability of future losses.

Annualization convention: 252 observations per year. Assumed annual risk-free rate: 2.00%. Results describe the supplied historical sample and are not forecasts or investment advice.
