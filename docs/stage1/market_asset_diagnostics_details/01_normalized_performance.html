What this chart measures

Compares the historical growth of a common starting value of 100 for every asset.

Why it matters for portfolio research

Makes price growth comparable across assets with different starting prices.

Construction and formula

Normalized value = price at date / first aligned price × 100.

Calculated finding

AAPL had the highest total return (1370.01%); BND had the lowest (20.41%).

Assumptions and limitations

Price growth is not a forecast. Interpretation as investor total return depends on whether the input adjusted prices correctly reflect distributions and corporate actions.

Annualization convention: 252 observations per year. Assumed annual risk-free rate: 2.00%. Results describe the supplied historical sample and are not forecasts or investment advice.
