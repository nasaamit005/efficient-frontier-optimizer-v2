6. Extreme-Return Diagnostics

Review dates with returns more than three sample standard deviations from the mean.

What this chart measures

Flags returns whose absolute distance from their asset's sample mean exceeds three sample standard deviations.

Why it matters for portfolio research

Identifies dates for data-quality review and potential event investigation.

Construction and formula

Z-score = (daily return − sample mean) / sample standard deviation; flag when absolute Z-score exceeds 3.

Calculated finding

The rule flagged 265 asset-day observations. Flags are not proof of errors.

Assumptions and limitations

Financial returns may have heavy tails and changing volatility. This screen is not a universal outlier test. Observations are not automatically deleted.

Annualization convention: 252 observations per year. Assumed annual risk-free rate: 2.00%. Results describe the supplied historical sample and are not forecasts or investment advice.
