Limitations

  • Historical mean/covariance estimates are noisy and sensitive to the sample period.
  • No transaction costs, taxes, slippage, liquidity constraints, or rebalancing execution effects are included.
  • Bootstrap estimates use a circular moving-block method and a finite number of replications; increase replications for publication-quality inference.
  • Optimized portfolio results are sensitive to the 2% risk-free rate and 40% weight cap.
  • Survivorship and point-in-time universe effects are not addressed by this fixed current-asset universe.
